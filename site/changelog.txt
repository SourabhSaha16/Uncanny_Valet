## cars: bouncy car-on-car collisions with a bump sound
Date: 2026-11-10
Files: Assets/_Project/Scripts/Cars/{CarBounceRule,CarBumpLimiter,CarCollisionResponse,CarHandlingKit}.cs (new), Assets/_Project/Scripts/Cars/{CarDriver,CarFactory,CarHandlingConfig}.cs, Assets/_Project/Scripts/Core/Events/CarEvents.cs, Assets/_Project/Scripts/Audio/{SfxId,SfxAlerts,SfxRecipeBook,SfxEventRouter}.cs, Assets/_Project/Data/Cars/CarHandlingConfig.asset, Assets/_Project/Scenes/Bootstrap.unity, tests (EditMode Cars/CarBounceRuleTests, Audio/CarBumpSfxTests, PlayMode/CarBounceTests)
Why: the human asked for bouncing collisions between cars. An explicit rule, not a PhysicsMaterial2D, because Box2D restitution would also bounce cars off walls and cannot treat braked parked cars as heavy. CarCollisionResponse (one per car) runs in OnCollisionEnter2D and replaces the car's own speed along the contact normal with a 1D restitution result computed from both cars' pre-impact velocities (CarDriver.PreSolveVelocity, the velocity set before the physics step), so mass differences (EV 1.5x later) work. A car with nobody in it counts ParkedMassScale (8) times heavier: the driven car rebounds, the parked one only shuffles and settles under its drag. Rule for who takes part: both cars must have an enabled CarDriver and a Dynamic body. CarAutoDrive and trips switch the body to Kinematic, the collider off and the driver off, so arriving, departing and queued cars neither trigger nor suffer the response and are not jittered; walls, the valet and pedestrians are unchanged. CarsCollided(WorldX, WorldY, Strength01) is a new additive Core event published by the lower-id car of the pair, once per pair per 0.25 s (CarBumpLimiter), only above 0.5 u/s approach speed; strength is approach speed 0.5..6 mapped to 0..1. Audio: SfxId.CarBump, a 0.25 s falling sine thud plus a muffled knock (SfxAlerts.CarBump), SfxEventRouter plays it with its own 0.1 s SfxRateLimiter. Values (CarHandlingConfig): Bounciness 0.4, MinImpactSpeed 0.5, FullImpactSpeed 6, ParkedMassScale 8, BumpPairInterval 0.25. A driven car hitting a parked one at 3 u/s rebounds about 0.7 u/s and shoves it about 0.06 units.
