## blessings: tips become blessings, favour, curses, lot threshold [Claude]

Why: Issue #81 of epic #79. Tip, combo, complaint and score become blessing, favour, curse and blessings everywhere (types, events, labels, comments); the formula and numbers are unchanged except that the Perfect Park bonus is gone (perfect events only fill the power bar). Stars are removed: the lot has a blessing threshold (LotDefinition.BlessingThreshold, registered as ILotGoal when the lot starts) and at shift end ShiftBlessings publishes ShiftResultReady, then LotCleared or LotFailed; the results screen shows blessings, threshold, CLEARED or FAILED, curses and peak favour. The HUD shows the bolt, the blessing count, a bar with a threshold marker, favour as a bare multiplier and a bolt on the pop. Renamed serialized fields keep their values through FormerlySerializedAs.
Tests: EditMode and PlayMode all pass (ShiftBlessingsTests, ShiftBlessingsLotTests, FavourTests, LotJudgementTests, HudRulesTests, HudLayoutTests, ResultsScreenTests, RunProgressTests, BootstrapSceneSmokeTests and the rest); RunBatch run.
