Why: Issue #121 of epic #79. The hard-coded dirty and EV traits become two `ServiceModifierDefinition` assets (Muddy, cleared at the Wash station kind; NeedsCharge, cleared at Charging, starts 20% charged) on a list-based car state: `ModifierSet` in requests, events and snapshots, a list of `ModifierInstance` on each ticket with its own progress. Events `CarChargeChanged/CarFullyCharged/CarCleanChanged/CarCleaned` became `ModifierProgressChanged/ModifierCleared`, `TicketTraitsAssigned` became `TicketModifiersAssigned`. Arrival chances come from the lot (`LotDefinition` list of modifier and chance, seeded with the other per-lot pacing values before the dials; roll order is list order, one draw, at most one modifier per car); the two Car Mix dials and the shift plan's EV and wash chances are removed (their TuningKey numbers stay reserved as Retired...). Each modifier has its own pixel icon, word forms for the key board status (Washing, Washed, Charging, Charged) and clear time and bonus keys. A car holding two modifiers is tracked, cleared and reported per modifier (MuddyEv cars already do). The word Dirty is gone from code, UI and F2 labels (CarArchetype Dirty and DirtyEv are now Muddy and MuddyEv; DirWeightDirty and DirWeightDirtyEv are DirWeightMuddy and DirWeightMuddyEv, same numbers; the director profile fields keep their values through FormerlySerializedAs).
Tests: EditMode 1350/1350 (the old suite migrated, plus ModifierTests, LotModifierChanceTests, a two-modifier ticket, snapshot, workload and tip test, dial removal and key-number checks; the 200-seed Director soak test passes), PlayMode 30/30 (BootstrapSceneSmokeTests plus two new tests on the real training lot: catalog and chances registered and seeded, and a Muddy plus NeedsCharge car washed and charged end to end). RunBatch run: only the two new definition assets and the lots' chance lists changed; Bootstrap.unity, Lot_MidgardCrazy.unity and CarHandlingConfig.asset churn reverted.
