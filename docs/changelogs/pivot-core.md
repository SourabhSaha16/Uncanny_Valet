Why: chaos_redesign.md sections 3, 8 and 9. The mafia layer (debt, strikes, boss orders, phone UI, multi-night plan) is cut. A shift is 4 minutes from a ShiftConfig asset (length, star thresholds 150/300/450, $15 complaint penalty, combo cap 4x, pacing plan). ShiftClock counts real seconds down and publishes ShiftStarted, ShiftTick per game minute, ShiftLast30Seconds and ShiftEnded behind IGameClock. ShiftScore owns score, combo and complaints: a tip adds to the score and raises the combo, a $0 tip resets it, and either ticket timer running out (an angry patron) costs the penalty and resets it. The tip is base + speed + patience bonus + charge + clean, less damage stages, times the combo. Damage is an input defaulting to 0; nothing produces damage yet. Game minutes now default to one per real second, so patron windows (park 45, pickup 60, stay 20-45) read as seconds; arrivals are every 15-25 s. The Night assembly keeps its name (UncannyValet.Night) to avoid asmdef churn. The results screen shows stars, score and one Retry button (click, Enter or Space) that publishes RetryShiftRequested; ShiftFlow restarts the shift from a clean lot.
Tests: added or rewrote EditMode tests for the shift timer and clock, plan source and live tuning, combo, stars, damage stages, patience bonus, complaint rules, the full shift loop, shift flow and results text. NOT run: Unity could not be launched in this sandbox, so compilation and the whole suite are unverified.
