## cars: bouncy car-on-car collisions with a bump sound
Date: 2026-11-10
Files: Assets/_Project/Scripts/Cars/{CarBounceRule,CarBumpLimiter,CarCollisionResponse,CarHandlingKit}.cs (new), Assets/_Project/Scripts/Cars/{CarDriver,CarFactory,CarHandlingConfig}.cs, Assets/_Project/Scripts/Core/Events/CarEvents.cs, Assets/_Project/Scripts/Audio/{SfxId,SfxAlerts,SfxRecipeBook,SfxEventRouter}.cs, Assets/_Project/Data/Cars/CarHandlingConfig.asset, Assets/_Project/Scenes/Bootstrap.unity, tests (EditMode Cars/CarBounceRuleTests, Audio/CarBumpSfxTests, PlayMode/CarBounceTests)
Why: the human asked for bouncing collisions between cars. An explicit rule, not a PhysicsMaterial2D, because Box2D restitution would also bounce cars off walls and cannot treat braked parked cars as heavy. CarCollisionResponse (one per car) runs in OnCollisionEnter2D and replaces the car's own speed along the contact normal with a 1D restitution result computed from both cars' pre-impact velocities (CarDriver.PreSolveVelocity, the velocity set before the physics step), so mass differences (EV 1.5x later) work. A car with nobody in it counts ParkedMassScale (8) times heavier: the driven car rebounds, the parked one only shuffles and settles under its drag. Rule for who takes part: both cars must have an enabled CarDriver and a Dynamic body. CarAutoDrive and trips switch the body to Kinematic, the collider off and the driver off, so arriving, departing and queued cars neither trigger nor suffer the response and are not jittered; walls, the valet and pedestrians are unchanged. CarsCollided(WorldX, WorldY, Strength01) is a new additive Core event published by the lower-id car of the pair, once per pair per 0.25 s (CarBumpLimiter), only above 0.5 u/s approach speed; strength is approach speed 0.5..6 mapped to 0..1. Audio: SfxId.CarBump, a 0.25 s falling sine thud plus a muffled knock (SfxAlerts.CarBump), SfxEventRouter plays it with its own 0.1 s SfxRateLimiter. Values (CarHandlingConfig): Bounciness 0.4, MinImpactSpeed 0.5, FullImpactSpeed 6, ParkedMassScale 8, BumpPairInterval 0.25. A driven car hitting a parked one at 3 u/s rebounds about 0.7 u/s and shoves it about 0.06 units.
Tests: EditMode 910/910 passed (new: CarBounceRule and limiter 12, bump sfx 3); PlayMode 11/11 passed (3 new: rebound plus event plus settle, gentle touch ignored, non-live car ignored). Not verified by hand in play: the sound level and the feel of the rebound.

## cars: skid marks and tyre audio
Date: 2026-11-10
Files: Assets/_Project/Scripts/Cars/SlipReading.cs (new), Assets/_Project/Scripts/Cars/Fx/{SkidConfig,SkidSettings,SkidState,SkidRule,SkidEmitter,DecalLedger,DecalPool,MarkSegment,TyreTrail,TyreMarkMath,SlipMeter}.cs (new), Assets/_Project/Scripts/Cars/{CarDriver,CarFactory,CarHandlingConfig,CarHandlingLive,DriftModel}.cs, Assets/_Project/Scripts/Core/Contracts/{ISlipSource,HandlingRows}.cs, Assets/_Project/Scripts/Core/Events/{CarEvents,TuningKey}.cs, Assets/_Project/Scripts/Audio/{SfxTyres,SquealLoop,SquealMix,TyreAudio}.cs (new), Assets/_Project/Scripts/Audio/{AudioConfig,AudioDirector,SfxEventRouter,SfxId,SfxRecipeBook}.cs, Assets/_Project/Data/Cars/CarHandlingConfig.asset, tests (EditMode Cars/{SkidRuleTests,DecalLedgerTests,TyreMarkTests,CarHandlingTuningTests}, Audio/TyreSoundTests; PlayMode/SkidMarkTests)
Why: car_handling_spec.md section 6 and lead notes 7 and 12 (rollout step 3). CarDriver exposes a read-only SlipReading (vF, vL, throttle, Shift) per FixedUpdate, taken before that step's damping; SkidRule (pure, one per car) turns it into sliding (|vL| > 1.2, or Shift above 2 u/s; ends below 75% of the threshold), intensity from DriftModel's slip angle (40 deg = full) and mark opacity (30% to 80%), and a hard-brake start (throttle against travel above 4 u/s). SkidEmitter is a thin shell on every car, active only with a seated driver and an enabled CarDriver (trip and auto-driven cars never emit): it drops a stroke behind each rear tyre every 1.0 unit of tyre travel, lying from the tyre's previous to its current position (the true travel direction), into DecalPool (cap 40, fade 6 s, oldest recycled, per-instance colour with a tint parameter for blood, shared 1x1 sprite, sorting order -3: above asphalt -25/-24 and route and curb arrows -5/-4, below traffic 1, pedestrians 4 and cars 9+). Events (additive, Core): CarSlideStarted, CarSlideEnded, CarHardBraked. The squeal reads slip strength through the new Core contract ISlipSource (IsSliding, Intensity01), polled by Audio each frame: no event per frame and no allocation; CarFactory registers its SlipMeter in Services at Start (Bootstrap is not edited). Audio: SfxId.TyreChirp (0.15 s rising high chirp, rate limited 0.25 s) on CarSlideStarted, SfxId.TyreScreech (0.45 s, rate limited 0.6 s) on CarHardBraked, and a 0.4 s seamless squeal loop (whole cycles per tone, circular high-passed hiss) on a dedicated AudioSource driven by TyreAudio and the pure SquealMix (volume 0.25..0.8 and pitch 0.9..1.5 with intensity, 0.03 s attack, 0.15 s fade-out, fades while paused). F2 keys under Car handling (advanced): slide threshold, Shift skid speed, mark darkness, mark fade time; the other skid numbers (SkidConfig, serialized inside CarHandlingConfig so no new asset) and the decal cap are fixed. Mark spacing is 1.0 unit and stroke width 0.07 (one art pixel) because a 40-decal cap with short strokes would recycle in under a second.
Tests: EditMode 948/948 passed (31 new: SkidRule 11, decals and tyre trail 10, slip meter 1, tuning 1, audio 8); PlayMode 14/14 passed (3 new: driven slide leaves marks and reports the slide, slide ends when the driver gets out, driverless car never emits). Not verified by hand: mark visibility at zoom 0.78, sound character, loop seam in WebGL.
